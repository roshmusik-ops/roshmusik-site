Directory FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Public sources — primarily Google Maps business listings — verified manually before delivery. No private DBs, no LinkedIn scraping.

How fresh is the data?

Re-scraped every 30 days. Pro subscribers get a fresh CSV monthly with new businesses added and dead numbers removed.

What if numbers don't work?

If more than 10% of phone numbers bounce, we re-deliver a fresh batch free. We aim for <5%.

Can I get a category not listed?

Yes. Custom scrapes start at ₹3,000. Email rosh.musik@gmail.com.

Is this legal?

All data is publicly available business contact info. You're responsible for following DND / spam laws in your outreach.
